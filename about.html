About Student Toolkit

Free and simple online tools designed to help students with everyday academic calculations and tasks.

What Is Student Toolkit?

Student Toolkit is a free educational website that provides useful online calculators and resources for students. Our goal is to make common academic calculations simple, quick, and easy to understand.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide students with simple and accessible tools that can help them save time and better understand their academic information.

We aim to keep our tools easy to use so students can get useful results without complicated steps or unnecessary registration.

What We Offer

Student Toolkit currently provides several free student calculators, including:

  • Percentage Calculator
  • Age Calculator
  • GPA Calculator
  • Grade Calculator
  • Attendance Calculator

Why Student Toolkit?

Students often need to calculate percentages, grades, GPA, attendance, and other academic information. Student Toolkit brings these useful tools together in one convenient place.

Our calculators are designed to be straightforward and accessible on both desktop and mobile devices.

Our Commitment

We are committed to improving Student Toolkit by adding useful student tools and resources over time. We also aim to keep the website simple, helpful, and easy to use.

Thank You

Thank you for visiting Student Toolkit. We hope our free tools make your academic tasks a little easier.
