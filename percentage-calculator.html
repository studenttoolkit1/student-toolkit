Free Online Percentage Calculator

Our free percentage calculator helps students calculate percentages quickly and easily. You can find a percentage of a number, determine what percentage one number is of another, and calculate percentage increase or decrease.

How to Calculate a Percentage

To calculate a percentage of a number, multiply the percentage by the number and divide by 100. For example, 20% of 200 is 40.

Percentage Increase and Decrease

The percentage change calculator helps you find how much a value has increased or decreased compared with its original value. This can be useful for students when comparing marks, scores, prices, or other numerical values.
