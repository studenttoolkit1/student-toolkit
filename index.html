Percentage Calculator

Our free percentage calculator helps students quickly calculate percentages, find what percentage one number is of another, and calculate percentage increase or decrease.

What is a Percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. Percentages are commonly used in mathematics, exams, grades, discounts, statistics, and everyday calculations.

How to Calculate a Percentage

To calculate a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage and divide the result by 100.

Percentage of a Number = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

How to Use Our Percentage Calculator

  1. Choose the type of percentage calculation you need.
  2. Enter the required numbers.
  3. Click the Calculate button.
  4. View your percentage result.

Percentage Increase or Decrease

Our percentage calculator can also help you find the percentage change between an original value and a new value. This can be useful for comparing scores, prices, marks, and other numbers.

Why Use a Percentage Calculator?

A percentage calculator saves time and helps students perform percentage calculations quickly and easily without doing the calculations manually.

What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is a simple online tool that calculates a person's age based on their date of birth. It can show your age in years, months, and days, making it easy to find your exact age.

How Does an Age Calculator Work?

An age calculator compares your date of birth with a selected date and calculates the difference between the two dates. This allows you to find your age accurately in years, months, and days.

How to Calculate Your Age

To calculate your age manually, subtract your birth date from the date on which you want to know your age. Because months have different numbers of days, calculating an exact age manually can sometimes be difficult.

How to Use Our Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Select the date on which you want to calculate your age.
  3. Click the "Calculate Age" button.
  4. View your exact age in years, months, and days.

Why Use an Age Calculator?

Our age calculator makes it easy to calculate your exact age without performing date calculations manually. It can be useful for students, personal records, applications, forms, and everyday date calculations.

GPA Calculator

Our free GPA calculator helps students calculate their grade point average using course credits and grades. Enter your subjects, credits, and grades to quickly calculate your GPA.

What is GPA?

GPA stands for Grade Point Average. It is a numerical representation of your academic performance based on the grades you receive in your courses. GPA is commonly used by schools, colleges, and universities to measure student performance.

How to Calculate GPA

To calculate GPA, multiply each course's grade point by its credit value. Add all the grade points together and divide the total by the number of credits.

GPA = Total Grade Points ÷ Total Credits

How to Use Our GPA Calculator

  1. Enter the name of each course.
  2. Enter the credit value for each course.
  3. Select the grade you received.
  4. Click "Add Subject" to add more courses if needed.
  5. Click "Calculate GPA" to see your GPA.

GPA Calculation Example

For example, if you receive different grades across several courses, each grade is multiplied by its course credits. The total grade points are then divided by the total number of credits to calculate your GPA.

Why Use a GPA Calculator?

A GPA calculator can help students quickly check their academic performance, understand their current grade point average, and plan their studies more easily.

Attendance Calculator

Our free attendance calculator helps students calculate their attendance percentage based on total classes and classes attended. Quickly check your attendance and understand how many classes you need to attend.

What is an Attendance Calculator?

An attendance calculator is a simple tool that calculates your attendance percentage using the number of classes you attended and the total number of classes held.

How to Calculate Attendance Percentage

Attendance percentage is calculated by dividing the number of classes attended by the total number of classes and multiplying the result by 100.

Attendance Percentage = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes) × 100

How to Use Our Attendance Calculator

  1. Enter the total number of classes.
  2. Enter the number of classes you attended.
  3. Click the calculate button.
  4. View your attendance percentage.

Why Use an Attendance Calculator?

An attendance calculator helps students quickly check their attendance percentage and keep track of their class attendance. It can also help students understand their current attendance before exams or important academic requirements.
