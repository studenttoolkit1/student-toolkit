Free Online Grade Calculator

Student Toolkit's free grade calculator helps students calculate their percentage and find their grade from marks obtained and total marks. It provides a quick and simple way to check academic results without performing the calculation manually.

How to Calculate Your Percentage

To calculate your percentage, divide the marks obtained by the total marks and multiply the result by 100.

Percentage = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100

For example, if you obtain 80 marks out of 100, your percentage is 80%.

How to Use Our Grade Calculator

Using the grade calculator is simple. Enter the marks you obtained, enter the total possible marks, and click the Calculate Grade button. The calculator will calculate your percentage and display the corresponding grade.

  1. Enter your marks obtained.
  2. Enter the total possible marks.
  3. Click the Calculate Grade button.
  4. View your calculated percentage and grade.

Why Use a Grade Calculator?

A grade calculator can save time and make it easier to understand your academic performance. Students can quickly calculate their percentage and check their grade without doing the calculation manually.

It can be useful when checking exam marks, test scores, assignments, or other academic results.

Who Can Use This Grade Calculator?

Grade Calculation

This calculator uses the grading scale built into Student Toolkit to determine the displayed grade from the calculated percentage. Percentage and Grade Example

Suppose a student receives 72 marks out of 100. The percentage is calculated as:

(72 ÷ 100) × 100 = 72%
