Free Online Age Calculator

Student Toolkit's free age calculator helps you calculate your exact age from your date of birth. The calculator can show the difference between your birth date and a selected date in years, months, and days.

How Does an Age Calculator Work?

An age calculator compares a person's date of birth with a selected date. It calculates the difference between the two dates and presents the result as years, months, and days.

For example, if a person was born on January 15, 2010, the calculator can determine their age on a later date by calculating the exact difference between those two dates.

How to Use the Age Calculator

Using this age calculator is simple. Enter your date of birth in the first field, select the date you want to calculate your age at in the second field, and then click the Calculate Age button.

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Select the date you want to calculate your age at.
  3. Click the Calculate Age button.
  4. View your age in years, months, and days.

Why Use an Age Calculator?

Calculating an exact age manually can be difficult when the dates include different months or leap years. An online age calculator can make the calculation faster and easier.

Students and other users can use an age calculator when checking school forms, applications, records, important dates, or simply finding their exact age.

What Information Do You Need?

You only need two dates to use this calculator: your date of birth and the date at which you want to calculate your age. The calculator then calculates the difference between those dates.

Can I Calculate My Age on a Specific Date?

Yes. Enter your date of birth and choose any valid date in the Calculate Age At field. The calculator can then determine your age on that selected date.
