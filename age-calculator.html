Free Online Age Calculator

Our free age calculator helps you calculate your exact age from your date of birth. It can show your age in years, months, and days quickly and easily.

How Does an Age Calculator Work?

An age calculator compares your date of birth with a selected date and calculates the difference between the two dates. This makes it easy to find your exact age.

How to Use the Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth, choose the date you want to calculate your age at, and click the Calculate Age button. The calculator will display your age in years, months, and days.

Why Use an Age Calculator?

An online age calculator can save time when you need to calculate an exact age for school forms, applications, personal records, or everyday date calculations.
