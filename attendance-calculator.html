Free Online Attendance Calculator

Student Toolkit's free attendance calculator helps students calculate their attendance percentage using the total number of classes and the number of classes attended. It provides a quick and simple way to check attendance without performing the calculation manually.

How to Calculate Attendance Percentage

Attendance percentage can be calculated by dividing the number of classes attended by the total number of classes and multiplying the result by 100.

Attendance Percentage = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes) × 100

For example, if you attended 45 classes out of 50 total classes, your attendance percentage is 90%.

Why Use an Attendance Calculator?

An attendance calculator makes it easier to monitor your class attendance and understand your current attendance level. Students can quickly calculate their percentage instead of doing the calculation manually.

Keeping track of attendance can also help students understand whether they are meeting the attendance requirements of their school, college, university, or course.

Who Can Use This Attendance Calculator?

Students, teachers, and anyone who needs to calculate class attendance percentage can use this free online tool. It can be useful for schools, colleges, universities, courses, and other educational programs.

Attendance Percentage Example

Suppose you attended 45 classes out of 50 total classes. Using the attendance formula:

(45 ÷ 50) × 100 = 90%

Therefore, your attendance percentage would be 90%.
