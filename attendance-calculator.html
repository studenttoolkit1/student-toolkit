Free Online Attendance Calculator

Our free attendance calculator helps students calculate their attendance percentage using the total number of classes and the number of classes attended.

How to Calculate Attendance Percentage

Attendance percentage can be calculated by dividing the number of classes attended by the total number of classes and multiplying the result by 100.

Attendance Percentage = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes) × 100

How to Use Our Attendance Calculator

Enter the total number of classes and the number of classes you attended. Then click the Calculate Attendance button. The calculator will show your attendance percentage.

Why Use an Attendance Calculator?

An attendance calculator makes it easy to check your attendance percentage without doing the calculation manually. Students can use it to monitor their attendance and understand their current attendance level.

Who Can Use This Attendance Calculator?

Students, teachers, and anyone who needs to calculate class attendance percentage can use this free online tool.

Attendance Example

If you attended 45 classes out of 50 total classes, your attendance percentage would be 90%.
