What Is GPA?

GPA stands for Grade Point Average. It is a numerical representation of a student's academic performance. GPA is commonly calculated using grade points and course credits.

How to Calculate GPA

GPA can be calculated by dividing total grade points by total credits. The basic formula is:

GPA = Total Grade Points ÷ Total Credits

Why Use a GPA Calculator?
