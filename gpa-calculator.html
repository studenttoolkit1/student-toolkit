Student Toolkit's free GPA calculator helps students calculate their Grade Point Average using course credits and grades. Add your subjects, enter the credit value for each course, select the corresponding grade, and calculate your GPA.

What Is GPA?

GPA stands for Grade Point Average. It is a numerical representation of a student's academic performance. GPA is commonly calculated by combining grade points with the credit value of each course.

How Is GPA Calculated?

A weighted GPA is generally calculated by multiplying each course's grade points by its credits, adding the resulting grade points, and dividing the total by the total number of credits.

Formula: GPA = Total Quality Points ÷ Total Credits

For example, if your courses have different credit values, a course with more credits has a greater effect on your overall GPA.

4.0 GPA Scale

This calculator uses a 4.0 grading scale. The available grade values range from A+ and A at 4.0 points to F at 0.0 points, with intermediate grades assigned their corresponding grade-point values.

Grading systems can vary between schools, colleges, and universities. If your institution uses a different grading scale, compare its official grading policy with the values used by this calculator.

Why Use a GPA Calculator?

Calculating GPA manually can take time, especially when you have several courses with different credit values. A GPA calculator makes it easier to estimate your overall grade point average quickly.

Students can use this tool to check their current academic performance, estimate their semester GPA, or understand how different course grades may affect their average.

Who Can Use This GPA Calculator?
